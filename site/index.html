The model

One formula, five words

Point doesn't have features. It has objects and the capabilities that accept them — which is why adding an action never means redesigning a screen.

Object
A file plus cheap signals: kind, and features like has-text or zip-of-images.
Intent
Extract, transform or send — the three things a person actually wants.
Capability
What is possible. The UI knows only this — never how it is done.
Realizer
How. On device, in the cloud, on your PC — swappable without touching the UI.
Object
A new object with its own possibilities. The loop closes.
≤300ms
The first screen never waits
Actions appear from zero-cost signals only. Deeper reading of a PDF or archive happens after, and quietly adds more.
Inferred
The graph builds itself
Add a capability and the graph grows. Nobody maintains a map of what leads where — the contracts are the map.
Your copy
Nothing leaks, nothing lingers
The shared file is copied into a private scratch space immediately and wiped when the journey ends. AI runs only after you ask for it.

Entry points

Five ways in, no home screen needed

Point is a layer over the apps you already use. You reach it from wherever the object already is.

Share sheet
The main road. Share anything from any app — Point opens on the object itself.
Launcher
Recent objects. Reopen last week's receipt without sharing it again.
Selected text
Highlight text anywhere and send it to Point — literally the right-click.
Quick Settings
A tile for the clipboard and screenshots — two taps from anywhere.
QR to your PC
Scan once to pair a desktop. Then objects and the clipboard flow both ways.
Philosophy

One question, asked well

“What can I do with this object right now?”
Every feature is judged against that sentence. If it needs its own domain, its own state and its own navigation, it belongs in a different app.
What Point never becomes
A browser A task manager A CRM A notes app An AI chat
North star
Every release must reduce the number of times you switch apps.
A feature that doesn't shorten a cross-app path probably shouldn't ship. That's the whole ship / no-ship test.
Every action, a little joy
Even “Copy”. Nothing appears instantly — it is born. Objects breathe, bubbles are drawn out of them, understanding glows.
Never a spinner
While Point is thinking, it pulses light through the object. Motion explains the model — otherwise it isn't there.
Roadmap

From an app to a network of capabilities

Each phase reuses the seams of the previous one. Because the UI only knows what is possible, the answers to how can move to the cloud, to your desktop, or to somebody else's capability pack without a redesign.

01
Product — real actions, offline, history, brand
44 capabilities, 44 realizers, 9 enrichers, 108 test classes. The flow works end to end.
Done
02
A layer over Android
Reached through five real entry points instead of a floating bubble — share, launcher, selected text, tile, QR.
Mostly there
03
Platform — third-party capability packs
Declarative registration plus metadata (cost, latency, auth) as the basis for paid capabilities. Seams proven on OCR; no packs yet.
Seams ready
04
Internet Capability Graph
Actions are discovered in a network of capabilities rather than compiled into the app.
Designed
05
Liquid software — capabilities flow between devices
Objects already travel to your PC and back. Next: a capability declared on one device, executed on another.
