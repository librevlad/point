Give a file

Point uploads it and gives you a link. Send the link, or show it as a QR code to someone next to you. They open it in a browser, or straight in Point if they have it.

Get a file

Point shows a QR code and a link. The other person opens it in a browser and drops in the file. It lands in Point on your phone.

Works in any browserThe link lives for a day
