How it works

Three steps, no menus

1

Share it

Pick Point in the Share menu of any app. Or open it from the quick settings panel, from selected text, or from print.

2

Point looks closer

The first actions show up at once. While you look, Point finds more: text, numbers, addresses, codes.

3

Keep going

Each step gives you something new, and that has its own next steps. Go as far as you need.

Phone and computer

Continue on your computer

Sign in on both, and your phone and computer find each other.

Same object, bigger screen
