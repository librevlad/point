One place for whatever you just got

You only see the steps that fit the thing in front of you. Each result is a new file or a finished action, and you can keep going from it.

Text out of a picture

Screenshots, photos of signs, pages and receipts. Copy the text, find a word, take a table out of it.

Clean PDFs

Photograph a document and get a straight, white, readable scan. Join several into one PDF or split one into pages.

Numbers and addresses

Phones, emails, addresses and dates found inside become a call, a contact, a route or a calendar event.

Photos

Cut out the subject, blur or replace the background, hide a part before sending, strip the hidden location data.

QR codes

Read a QR code from a photo or a screenshot and open what it holds. Make one from a link or a piece of text.

Phone and computer

Send something to your computer in one tap and carry on there. The result comes back to the phone.

Hand over a file with a link or a QR code

No messenger, no cable. The other person needs only a browser.

Give a file

Point uploads it and gives you a link. Send the link, or show it as a QR code to someone next to you. They open it in a browser, or straight in Point if they have it.

Get a file

Point shows a QR code and a link. The other person opens it in a browser and drops in the file. It lands in Point on your phone.

Your files stay yours

Point does what it can on your own devices.

  • Nothing leaves without a yes. If a step needs an online service, Point asks first.
  • Nothing piles up. Point keeps a copy of what you shared for a day at most, then deletes it.
  • No account to start. Sign in only when you want to link your phone and computer.
